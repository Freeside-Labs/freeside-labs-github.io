  1. 01 Attitude guidance

    Slew planning and constrained maneuvers, agility and timeline studies, pointing and jitter budgets.

  2. 02 Estimation

    MEKF design; star-tracker, gyro and sun-sensor error models; alignment observability; Monte Carlo checked against covariance bounds.

  3. 03 Control

    Robust closed-loop design, flexible-body dynamics, stability and performance margins in MATLAB/Simulink.

  4. 04 Flight software

    Portable GNC code in Rust and C++ that runs the same in simulation and on target. Unit-tested, requirements-traced.

  5. 05 Simulation

    6-DOF closed-loop sims with environment models (IGRF, NRLMSISE-00), sensor and actuator models, batch Monte Carlo.

  6. 06 Systems & reviews

    GNC requirements, actuator sizing, momentum budgets, interface docs, PDR/CDR packages.
